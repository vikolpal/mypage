Cheese & Eggs (Frico Eggs)

Description

A simple dish made with melted cheese and eggs, served with bread.

Ingredients

Mandatory

  • 3 eggs
  • Cheese (quantity is difficult to specify)
  • Bread

Steps

  1. Slice the cheese and spread the slices across a preheated frying pan. Add a little oil if the cheese is not particularly fatty. Leave some space between the cheese slices for the eggs.
  2. Remove from the heat when the egg whites are cooked.

Enjoy your meal!
