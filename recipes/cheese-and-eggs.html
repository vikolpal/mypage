Cheese and Eggs (Frico Eggs)

Description

Ingredients

Mandatory

  • 3 eggs
  • Cheese (quantity is difficult to specify)
  • Bread

Additional

Steps

  1. Slice the cheese and spread it across a preheated frying pan (add a little oil if the cheese is not fatty). Leave some space between the cheese slices for the eggs.
  2. Remove from the heat when the egg whites are cooked.

Enjoy your meal!
